Understanding these results

Finding affected cases

Shows how well the model identifies less common outcomes, such as low birth weight or perinatal death.

Ability to distinguish outcomes

Shows how well the model separates cases with the outcome from cases without it. Higher values are generally better.

Affected cases identified

Shows the percentage of affected cases the model successfully identifies at the selected alert threshold.

Best-performing model

Highlights the model selected as the best within the current evaluation results.

These results measure model performance on test data. They do not represent the probability of an individual mother or baby experiencing a health outcome. Clinical decisions should always involve qualified healthcare professionals.

Results are retrieved from the shared AfyaNova model evaluation system.
